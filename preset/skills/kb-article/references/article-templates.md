# Article templates

## How-to

Step-by-step instructions for a task.

```
# How to [accomplish task]

[Overview: what this guide covers and when you'd use it]

## Prerequisites
- [What's needed first]

## Steps
### 1. [Action]
[Specific instruction]

### 2. [Action]
[Instruction]

## Verify it worked
[How to confirm success]

## Common issues
- [Issue]: [Fix]

## Related articles
- [Links]
```

- Start each step with a verb.
- Give the exact path: "Go to **Settings > Integrations > API Keys**".
- Say what the user should see after each step ("a green confirmation banner").

## Troubleshooting

Diagnose and fix one problem.

```
# [What the user sees]

## Symptoms
- [What the user observes]

## Cause
[Why it happens, briefly and without jargon]

## Solution
### Option 1: [Most likely fix]
[Steps]

### Option 2: [Alternative if option 1 doesn't work]
[Steps]

## Prevention
[How to avoid it]

## Still having issues?
[How to get help]
```

- Lead with symptoms; customers search for what they see.
- Most likely fix first. Keep a complex root cause simple for customers.

## FAQ

A quick answer to a common question.

```
# [Question, in the customer's words]

[Direct answer, 1-3 sentences]

## Details
[Context or nuance, if needed]

## Related questions
- [Link]
```

- Answer in the first sentence.
- If the answer needs a walkthrough, it's a how-to.
- Group related FAQs and link between them.

## Known issue

A known bug or limitation, with a workaround.

```
# Known issue: [Short description]

**Status:** [Investigating / Workaround available / Fix in progress / Resolved]
**Affected:** [Who or what]
**Last updated:** [Date]

## Symptoms
[What users experience]

## Workaround
[Steps, or "No workaround available"]

## Fix timeline
[Expected date or current status]

## Updates
- [Date]: [Update]
```

- Keep the status current. A stale known issue erodes trust fastest.
- When the fix ships, mark it resolved and keep it live for 30 days for customers still searching the old symptoms.

## Reference

Tables of options, limits, settings or API details. Lead with a one-line overview, group by what the customer looks up, and link to the how-to articles that use them.
